    How to read this tablePrice · Volume · Moving averages · Cap

    Price

    Last = latest Yahoo quote (local ccy; ~15 min delayed). Grey = quote time (Geneva) while open, else last close date. Price returns only (no dividends). Name colour = today's move (up / down). As of ….

    Volume

    Ratio = last ÷ N-day average (5/20/50). Last = today so far while a market is open. 1.0× = average day; > 1.5× highlighted. Grey figure = N-day avg volume.

    Moving averages & cap

    % vs N-day SMA: +% above , −% below . Mkt cap / EV in EUR (Yahoo; FX as of …). Yields in bp. 10Y yields: official closes (US Treasury, Bundesbank, Bank of England, SNB) drive the level, 1D/1M/1Y changes and spreads (yield difference × 100, in bp, same-day closes); CNBC live under the name is the intraday move only; rows tagged fallback use CNBC closes (France: no keyless official daily series).

      Markets
      Table: Yahoo Finance quotes and daily data via GitHub Actions (prices every 15 min in market hours). Deal flow: NewsWeb, MFN, Nasdaq Nordic, Investegate, Yahoo news and the FI insider register (weekday morning + midday/evening power runs). Nordic Power: EEX settlements, Nord Pool day-ahead prices and UMM, ENTSO-E Transparency day-ahead prices, SE4 wind/load/outages and reservoirs (Energy-Charts / Energi Data Service fallback), NVE hydro, Svenska kraftnät SE4 load/flows. Gas storage: GIE AGSI+ (Gas Infrastructure Europe), daily. Charts: TradingView widgets; exchanges TradingView won't embed for free (London, Hong Kong, TVC indices) get a simple chart drawn here from CNBC's delayed price bars. Treasury yields cannot be embedded, so the Rates chart uses bond ETFs and Table yield rows open a Yahoo 1Y chart. 10-year yields: daily closes, changes and spreads from the official publishers (US Treasury par yield curve, Deutsche Bundesbank, Bank of England IADB, SNB data portal); France has no keyless official daily series, so its closes come from CNBC (fallback) with the ECB monthly long-term rate as official reference. CNBC's last quote shows only the intraday move. Other US Treasury rows: Yahoo. Markets search uses a static symbol index (LSE/AIM, OMX Stockholm, Oslo, NYSE/Nasdaq, TSX/TSXV, HKEX; works offline). TradingView lookup is tried when reachable; exotic names may need EXCH:SYM or in-chart search.
