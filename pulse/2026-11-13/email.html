• Jensen Huang is out at Nvidia after the affairs story, and the fallout is spreading to Washington, where lawmakers are pressing Howard Lutnick. The bigger market question is policy: tougher curbs on AI chip sales to China look more likely under new leadership. Wall Street slipped with three stocks falling for every one that rose, even as AMD, Broadcom and Arista held up the hardware trade.
• Rates did the damage. Short and long Treasury yields rose sharply together, the dollar firmed against the euro, franc and yen, and gold and silver fell hard. When bonds sell off this broadly, the AI premium in equities gets harder to defend, a point the FT is pressing on the debt funding the build-out.
