• The midterm count points to a Democratic wave, and the overnight reaction is risk-off: global stocks and the dollar are sliding on fears that a new Congress turns against the AI build-out. State races were already being watched as a test of the backlash against data centres and their power bills.
• Tuesday's close told a calmer story. Chips bounced after Monday's rout while Treasury yields rose, so the market went into the vote leaning back toward tech. That is the position now being tested.

• The Riksbank decides this morning and Norges Bank tomorrow, with the krone already the weakest major currency on the day. The Bank of England follows on Thursday and US payrolls on Friday.
