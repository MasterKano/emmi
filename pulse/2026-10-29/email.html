9 · Special · EQT–Range
EQT buys Range Resources for about $13bn in stock, betting on Appalachian gas for data centres and LNG
EQT agreed on 26 Oct to buy Range in an all-stock deal, 0.987 EQT shares each, valuing Range's equity at about $12.8bn, a 21% premium, per Novi Labs; closing is due in Q1 2027. EQT's case is the sector's lowest cost base plus $325m of synergies rising above $600m, which it says take breakeven below $2/MMBtu. UBS, as reported second-hand, sees 4–7% EPS dilution in 2027–28.
Our view. The largest US gas producer is consolidating its home basin with stock. It pays off if data-centre and LNG demand arrives on time; if it is late, the dilution looks expensive.
Watch. the S-4 filing and vote dates, antitrust review, and Henry Hub this winter.
