Our view. Sunday's wind, far above normal for the time of year, pulled weekend prices down; Monday's jump is weekday demand coming back on top of that low base. The market is split in two: nuclear availability holds the north and Finland near the floor, and the bottlenecks south keep that power from reaching the zones that need it. Southern Norway, southern Sweden and Denmark are pricing off Germany, and with hydro running above normal there is no Nordic supply shortfall behind the spike; it is a transmission and continental-demand story.
