Our view. The sell-off is about valuations along the AI supply chain, not Nvidia's numbers: the beat was not enough to answer doubts about how long data-centre spending keeps rising, and a firm dollar is pulling foreign money out of Taiwan. Our view. Thursday's jump is a supply story. Wind has dropped below normal for the time of year in recent days, after a windier start to the month, nuclear availability is still well short of normal with Forsmark 3 out, and a TenneT limit on the German link cuts SE4's import room until tonight. Forsmark 3's return and any pickup in wind are what would bring Friday's prices down; hydro output near normal means there is no structural deficit behind the spike.
