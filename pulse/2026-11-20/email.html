Our view. The Nordic split is a supply story. Nuclear's return has flooded the north, where SE1, SE2 and Finland dropped back towards their monthly norms. The south stays tight: wind is far below normal for the time of year, and the TenneT limit on the DK1→DE link, in place until 29 Nov, cuts DK1's interconnection with Germany at a time when Denmark and SE4 trade close to German prices. Until wind picks up, the price gap between north and south stays wide; hydro running near normal means there is no wider deficit behind it.
