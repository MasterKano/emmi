IN THIS BRIEF  1 Monitor  ·  2 Maps  ·  3 Energy desk  ·  4 Nordic power  ·  5 Top 5 reads  ·  6 News  ·  7 Calendar
• Monday was the day the war premium came out of oil. Iran's supreme leader gave the Doha talks his backing, and crude and products had their sharpest one-day fall since the spring.
• The risk has not gone away. Reports of a US strike on Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz overnight put a small bid back under crude, and the no-strike pledge only runs to today's midterms.
• Tech is where the stress sits. Chip and optical-network names led the selling and Apple warned that record memory-chip costs will hit its margins. Europe rose, led by aerospace and miners, with the oil majors at the bottom.
• Today is US election day, with the Riksbank, Norges Bank and the Bank of England deciding over the next three days and US payrolls on Friday. A midterm surprise would land on a market already short of tech leadership.
