1. Nordic Renewables and Power
Statnett · unlisted
Statnett halted new grid capacity reservations for data centres until 1 August 2027 at the latest, with 800 megawatts (MW) of data centres connected, 4,200 MW reserved, 9,100 MW in the queue and requests for 18,000 MW more, against peak Norwegian demand of just over 25,000 MW. The government plans a licensing scheme for existing and new data centres that excludes crypto mining, with its main principles due in the autumn (Mon 9 Nov).
Existing connections and sites with approved technical requirements can still connect, so projects already holding capacity gain scarcity value while new Norwegian sites stall until the rules are set.
