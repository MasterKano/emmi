SNM.OL · 1 year
A drone strike on Sunday night halted production at the Khor Mor gas field, one of the Kurdistan Region's largest, taking out about 2.8 gigawatts of the region's power generation; operator Dana Gas has given no restart date (press-reported). Separately, the Patriotic Union of Kurdistan (PUK) withdrew from the Kurdistan Regional Government (KRG) on Thursday and threatened a parallel administration in Sulaimani.
Neither touches ShaMaran's oil fields directly, but both raise security and political risk in the region that produces all its output.

DNO confirmed its cash offer for Capricorn Energy has lapsed after the shareholder meetings were not held by the 7 November deadline. Capricorn's board had switched its recommendation to Genel Energy's higher offer of about $436 million.
This clears the way for Genel's scheme, which still needs one Egyptian regulatory condition and is expected to complete in the fourth quarter.

Gulf Keystone received $16.5 million net for its first top-up cargo, which reconciles September to December 2025 Kurdistan export sales to international prices and recovers more than half of the $31.3 million owed for the period. It expects the rest by the end of November and had cash of $66.0 million on 6 November, before its $175 million bond settles on 10 November.
The same top-up mechanism applies to ShaMaran's Kurdistan exports, so this is the first sign the back payments are flowing.
