MTRS.ST · 1 year
Munters will invest USD 9.5 million for 45% of a coil-making joint venture with heat-exchanger maker Lordan near its Data Center Technologies plant in Selma, Texas, with a seven-year supply commitment and production in 12 to 18 months. It is also spending about EUR 25 million to expand its northern Italian chiller plant to meet demand in Europe (announced yesterday).
Both moves add cooling capacity for data centres, Munters' fastest-growing business, on the US and European sides.
European Energy · unlisted
European Energy's Australian arm signed an exclusivity agreement with data-centre operator AirTrunk on a 260 megawatt (MW) solar project, an 800+ megawatt-hour battery and up to 350 MW of wind in New South Wales' Hunter region, with a long-term power purchase agreement (PPA) still under discussion; solar and battery are due in 2030 and wind in 2032.
