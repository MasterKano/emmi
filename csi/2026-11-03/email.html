IPCO.TO · 1 year
International Petroleum Corporation (IPC) reported third-quarter net production of about 44,000 barrels of oil equivalent per day (boepd), in line with guidance, with free cash flow of $27 million and net debt down to $473 million; Blackrod Phase 1 is ramping up on plan towards its 30,000 barrels a day plateau in 2027. It raised full-year operating cash flow guidance to $275 to 350 million, on Brent at $80 to 110, from $230 to 330 million, kept production guidance at 44,000 to 47,000 boepd and will seek to renew its share buyback into 2027.
The higher cash-flow guide comes from the higher oil price assumption rather than more barrels; the Blackrod ramp-up to plateau is the next thing to watch.
