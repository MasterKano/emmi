Statistics Norway · unlisted
Statistics Norway, on Monday, reported record goods imports of NOK 108.2 billion in October, driven by data-centre equipment and cars: office machines and data-processing equipment more than doubled from a year earlier to NOK 8.7 billion, and data-storage imports reached about NOK 48 billion so far this year, up nearly 64%.
This is the build-out already under way; with Norway pausing new grid reservations for data centres, the pipeline behind it is what to watch in the next few months' figures.
