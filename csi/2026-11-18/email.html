ENQ.L · 1 month
   
EnQuest agreed to sell its UK upstream business to NEO NEXT+ for up to $960 million ($840 million cash on completion plus up to $120 million contingent): Magnus, Kraken and the Greater Kittiwake Area (operated), Golden Eagle (non-operated), the undeveloped Bressay field and Bentley discovery, plus its Sullom Voe Terminal interest and the Northern Leg Gas and Ninian pipelines. The assets produced 31,122 barrels of oil equivalent a day net in 2025, about $2.1 billion of decommissioning liabilities go with them, and completion is targeted for the first quarter of 2027.
EnQuest becomes a South East Asia producer of about 70,000 barrels a day once its four Malaysian acquisitions close on 31 December, with unit operating costs falling to about $11 a barrel; one of the last big UK independents leaves the North Sea.
