NKT kept its 2026 outlook and now expects operational EBITDA (earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation) at the upper end of its EUR 400-430 million range; third-quarter operational EBITDA was EUR 111 million (EUR 119 million a year earlier) as revenue at standard metal prices fell 7% organically against a high base. FWZ.TO · 1 month
   
Fireweed Metals, on Monday, was conditionally approved for up to C$14.0 million from Natural Resources Canada to take its Mactung tungsten project on the Yukon-Northwest Territories border toward a final investment decision (FID), subject to final due diligence; the money pays for metallurgical testing, engineering to a tender-ready stage and a study of a possible ammonium paratungstate (APT) processing plant. It comes on top of up to C$12.9 million agreed in August 2025 for planning the North Canol road and power corridor, and carries no commercial covenants on future tungsten sales.
Ottawa calls Mactung one of the world's largest undeveloped high-grade tungsten deposits; the feasibility study is due in early 2027 (press-reported), and CBC reported First Nation concern that the funding came before community consent, so consent and permitting are what to watch.
