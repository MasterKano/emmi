AKRBP.OL · 1 month
  
Norway's Supreme Court ruled on Friday, by six votes to five, that the government's approvals of the development plans for Equinor's Breidablikk and Aker BP's Tyrving and Yggdrasil were invalid, because emissions from burning the oil and gas, alone and with other Norwegian fields, were not properly assessed; the operators' permits do not lapse and the Energy Ministry must re-examine the decisions.
Breidablikk and Tyrving keep producing and Aker BP still expects Yggdrasil on stream next summer; the wider effect is a stricter climate assessment for every future plan for development and operation (PDO), and the green groups are weighing suits against the operators themselves (press-reported).
