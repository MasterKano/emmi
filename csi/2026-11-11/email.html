Vestas reported third-quarter revenue up 6% to EUR 5.7 billion with an EBIT margin before special items of 8.5% (7.8%) and record earnings per share of EUR 1.14, while firm turbine orders fell 27% to 3,377 megawatts (MW). LUN.TO · 1 year
The Chilean Copper Commission (Cochilco) said Escondida produced 93,800 tonnes of copper in September, down 21% year on year, while Codelco fell 14.1% to 99,300 tonnes and Collahuasi 19.2% to 30,700 tonnes (press-reported, announced yesterday).
Relevant to Lundin Mining's Chilean operations, Candelaria and Caserones; it adds to Chile's weakest quarter in at least two decades and keeps the copper supply story tight.

Orcadian Energy 13.25 1d 0.0%
1m −8.6%YTD +6.0%
GBp · close 10 Nov
ORCA.L · 1 year
Orcadian Energy expects to double its stake in the P2650 Mid-North Sea High licence to 100% after partner Triangle Energy (UK) defaulted on a cash call, subject to North Sea Transition Authority (NSTA) approval. Its key shallow-gas prospect, Glenlough, holds an estimated 131 billion cubic feet recoverable at a 31% chance of success, and it will seek to extend Phase A beyond February 2027.
Full ownership gives Orcadian a clean licence to farm down, but the drilling still depends on finding a partner before the Phase A deadline.
